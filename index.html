Who We Are

Connecting People, Businesses and Possibilities

BOK Integrated Nigeria Limited is a trusted provider of comprehensive last-mile Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) deployment services, specializing in cabling, splicing, and customer router installation.

With over four years of industry experience, we have successfully partnered with leading companies like FiberOne Broadband, delivering high-quality, timely, and efficient network infrastructure services.

Our commitment to excellence ensures seamless connectivity for end-users and high customer satisfaction for our clients.

MISSION

To bridge the connectivity gap by delivering innovative, reliable, and cost-effective last-mile fiber deployment services that empower communities and businesses to thrive in a digital world.

VISION

To become a leading provider of FTTH deployment solutions, recognized for our unmatched quality, customer satisfaction, and contribution to advancing the digital transformation in Nigeria and beyond.

What We Do

End-to-End Fiber Deployment Solutions

We offer a comprehensive suite of last-mile deployment services tailored to meet all critical aspects of FTTH rollout.

Network Design and Planning

We assess existing network infrastructure and plan optimized fiber cable deployment routes using GIS technologies for accuracy and efficiency.

  • Assessment of existing network infrastructure.
  • GIS-driven route planning.
  • Spatial accuracy and deployment efficiency.

Cabling and Installation

We deploy fiber cables from the nearest Point of Presence (PoP) to customer premises, ensuring proper cable management, protection, and long-term durability.

  • Last-mile fiber cable deployment
  • Customer premises connections
  • Cable management and protection

Splicing and Testing

Our technicians perform high-precision fiber splicing and comprehensive testing using industry-standard tools to guarantee low signal loss and high performance.

  • High-precision fiber splicing
  • Signal transmission optimization
  • Comprehensive testing and validation
  • Low-loss performance assurance

Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) Deployment

We install, configure, and test customer routers to provide seamless connectivity while educating customers on basic troubleshooting.

  • Router installation
  • Configuration and activation
  • Connectivity testing
  • Customer troubleshooting guidance
